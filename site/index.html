The Dependency Machine

A dependency graph made visible as a mechanical proving course. Each station represents a candidate stage; joins wait for every required upstream stage, while independent branches can advance on their own.

Illustrative scenarios, not live pipeline verdicts. These two scenes use synthetic, versioned fixtures to demonstrate the machine's visual states. The production badge will consume only an authenticated, independently validated CiAi snapshot.

Dependency failed · gate held

A failed adapter blocks its downstream renderer. The independent Physics branch can still pass. The knight is struck down and the drawbridge remains closed.

All required stages passed · gate open

Every required stage has accepted evidence. The knight reaches the end of the course and the final drawbridge opens.
